        Talkative ↔ Quiet
        Formal ↔ Casual
        Honest ↔ Shady
        Patient ↔ Impatient

    These modify:
        line length
        chance to add extra comments
        blunt vs polite phrasing

    Track small flags:
        first meeting
        frequent customer
        haggler
        helped NPC before
        caused trouble in town

    5) Context Awareness
        React to the world:
            raining → “Bad weather for travel.”
            war nearby → “Steel prices are up.”
            shop is busy → shorter lines
            late night → “Make it quick, I’m closing.”

    weight preferences
    carry state

    8) Optional Flavor Add-Ons

Sometimes append a small extra:
personal comment
rumor
complaint
compliment
Example:
“What can I do for you? Shipment’s late again…”
Low chance, big payoff.

9) Imperfection & Subtext

Humans don’t speak optimally.

Occasionally:
be vague
dodge questions
trail off
repeat phrases
contradict themselves slightly
Not often — just enough to avoid robotic perfection.

10) Dialogue Templates with Slots

Templates:
“If you’re looking for {item_type}, I’ve got {quality} stock.”
Slots pull from context.
Cheap, powerful variety.
